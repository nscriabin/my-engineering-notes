# My Engineering Notes

> You only really know what you think by writing*.
>
> -[Emma Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Smith_(scholar))

*I came across this quote in an [article](https://www.economist.com/britain/2026/09/24/ai-often-makes-writing-worse-even-if-it-makes-it-better) by the Economist and it resonated with me. Putting my thoughts to ~~paper~~ markdown, helps me to organise them.

This site, as the name suggests, contains **my** engineering notes, from my career as a **data engineer**.

These notes are practical in nature. They are a result of building and operating data platforms and pipelines.

I am a big believer of humanity sharing knowledge, which is why this site is public and open source. 


Finally, I had the privilege to learn from many great engineers in my life. There are too many to list but you know who you are and I sincerely thank you!

# Site Structure

The site structure should be self-explanatory but to be explicit:

- [Tools](/docs/tools/python) - contains notes about specific tools which I used and continue to use, everything from [Python](/docs/tools/python) to [Terraform](/docs/tools/terraform).
- [Tech Handbook](/docs/tech-handbook/engineering-principles) - contains notes about various aspects of software/data engineering craft. Everything from high-level [engineering principles](/docs/tech-handbook/engineering-principles) to specific [API data source template](/docs/tech-handbook/templates/data-source-api) when building a data pipeline. If you have ever worked in a large tech team, you might have seen something similar.
- [Learning Log](/docs/learning-log) - as the name suggests, it is a log of what I have learned, mainly related to tech but may include non-tech things (if they are interesting/useful to share). I update it on monthly basis.
