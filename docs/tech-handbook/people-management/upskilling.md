# Upskilling

If you want to upskill in a certain area, the most effective way is:

1. Read the official docs (clarifying any questions with ChatGPT).
2. Ask the team to give you a quick overview. This is also an opportunity to clarify any questions/doubts.
3. Pick-up work related to the area of study. Real mastery will come only after practical application.

:::tip
Your probation period is the best time to learn. Expectation to deliver are low and everyone is willing to make time for knowledge sharing. Ask questions, read the docs, develop a personal learning plan. Use this time wisely!
:::

Re developing a personal learning plan. Go through your team's tech stack (perhaps start with the job description) and identify what needs to be learned.
