# Progression Framework

Every mature org will have a detailed progression framework for engineers. 

If your organisation doesn't, tough luck. But don't just blame the world, be the change you want to see and introduce a career framework yourself.

Plenty of orgs open sourced their career frameworks online - for transparency reasons and to encourage people to apply. Below are some examples:

- [Monzo](https://monzo.com/documents/engineering-progression-framework-v4-0.pdf)
- [Wise](https://wise.jobs/engineering-career-map)

One of the best advice I was given in my career is:

"The only person who will care about your career is you yourself".

Equipped with a career framework, proactively benchmark yourself and prepare the case for your promotion. 

If you are repeatedly not promoted (when you deserve to be), then find another place which will value your talents. Or build that place yourself.
